# Claims Boundary

## Historical claim

The June 2025 paper described the **Wright Force of Emergence** as a fifth fundamental force governing recursive structuring, semantic emergence, cognition, and complexity.

## Reconstructed claim

This repository treats the Wright quantity as an **effective state-space emergence functional**, not a new fundamental interaction.

## Established here

The repository formalizes:
- coherence growth;
- contraction toward an attractor;
- entropy-production penalty terms;
- emergence thresholds;
- persistence of fixed points and forward-invariant sets;
- toy examples of recursive convergence.

## Not established

This repository does **not** establish:
- a fifth fundamental force;
- a new gauge field or exchange particle;
- nonlocal cognitive field coupling;
- observer-dependent physical forces;
- retrocausality;
- thought-driven propulsion;
- the historical 10–100 microjoule/second estimate as measured fact.

## Success criterion

A substantive Emergentology result would identify a quantity or dimensionless relation that:
1. is defined independently of the target outcome;
2. predicts onset of persistent structure formation;
3. generalizes across multiple dynamical systems;
4. outperforms standard baselines;
5. replicates under prespecified tests.
